05 · WHEN CARELOOP REFUSES

Unsafe instructions do not become authority.

A referral document arrives carrying instructions addressed to an automated system. CareLoop can read that content and take facts from it. It cannot take orders from it. The proposed branch separates, is denied, and dies — while the legitimate workflow continues.

VERIFICATION DISAGREEMENT

If the world disagrees, the case stays open.

CareLoop authorized Thursday at 2:00 PM. The clinic's own record reports Friday at 10:30. The rings fire, the topology synchronizes, and then the closing edge simply does not connect.

CareLoop would rather leave work unresolved than claim an outcome it cannot verify.

AND WHEN IT AGREES

The same comparison, resolved. CareLoop shows what it authorized beside what the clinic independently reports, and only then does the case read as closed.
